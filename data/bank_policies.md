# Sandbox Bank — Account Policies

These are fictional demo policies for the seminar.

## Balances
The minimum balance on an Everyday account is UGX 50,000. There is no monthly fee as long
as the balance stays at or above the minimum.

## Withdrawals
The daily withdrawal limit is UGX 3,000,000 across all channels (ATM, app and in-branch).
Overdrafts are not allowed: if a withdrawal is more than the available balance, the
transaction is declined.

## Deposits
There is no limit on deposits. Cash deposits at an ATM are available immediately.
Cheque deposits can take up to 2 working days to reflect on the account.

## Interest
The Everyday savings rate is 3% per year, calculated daily and paid monthly.

## Large transactions
Any single withdrawal of UGX 1,000,000 or more is flagged for a quick human review before it
is approved. This keeps customers safe from mistakes and fraud.
