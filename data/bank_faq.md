# Sandbox Bank — Customer FAQ

Sandbox Bank is a fictional demo bank used for this seminar. Nothing here is real.

## Opening an account
You can open a Everyday account online in about 10 minutes. You need to be at least
18 years old and provide one photo ID. The first account has no opening fee.

## Online and mobile banking
Online banking is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. If the app is down for
maintenance, it is usually back within 30 minutes.

## Cards and PINs
A new debit card arrives within 5 working days. You can reset your PIN in the app
under Settings, or at any Sandbox Bank ATM.

## Customer support
Phone support is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, and Saturday 9:00 AM to
1:00 PM. Support is closed on Sundays and public holidays. You can as well call our number +25634567808

## ATMs
Sandbox Bank ATMs are free to use. Using another bank's ATM costs UGX 2,000 per withdrawal.
